How to play

  1. Sixteen words hide four groups of four.
  2. Select 4 words that belong together and press Submit.
  3. Right: the group is revealed. Wrong: you lose a life. One away means 3 of your 4 words were right.
  4. Lose all your lives and the remaining groups are revealed.
  5. Stuck? Tap the Spoiler box for a gentle hint. Solved groups sometimes come with a 💡 fun fact.

Shortcuts: Enter submit · Esc clear · R shuffle
